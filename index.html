# Current Role

Design Lead

Website, candidate and employer apps, employer platform and brand at a hiring platform with 5M+ talents and 60K+ organizations across Southeast Asia

  • A layout complaint was hiding a workflow problem. I restructured the employer workspace around how employers already screen candidates. Rolling out to new employers first, then 20% of existing ones
  • Designed the self-serve subscription (May 2025) and the monthly quota built on it. The test showed the trade-off: about twice as many employers bought a plan after hitting the limit, but fewer jobs were posted
  • The resume was the first barrier to applying. We made chat the first action and kept the CV for later. In the MVP, applications processed within a day went from 9.7% to 18.4%
  • Started the employer app as a pilot for existing employers to reply from their phone, with sign-up and payment left for later. I was PM and designer for the first version
  • Made applying one tap, but not bulk or auto apply, to get more applications without low-intent mass applying. A/B tested, then rolled out to all users
  • Joined in 2022 to launch Glints' app in Vietnam as both designer and PM: the candidate app, the employer web and the app's metrics
