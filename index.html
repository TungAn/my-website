# How I Work

Engineering Background

I started as a front-end developer, so I think about how a design will be built and work through edge cases with engineers before the design is final.

Data and Research

I use product data and research together: search logs, screen-size data, A/B test results, employer interviews and competitor benchmarks.

Written Proposals

I write design proposals that lay out the problem, the evidence and the options, so PMs, engineers and leadership can decide together.
