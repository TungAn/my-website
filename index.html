# Current Role

Design Lead

A hiring platform in Southeast Asia with 5M+ talents and 60K+ organizations. I work across its website, candidate and employer apps, employer platform and brand, and have managed up to three designers.

  • Employer platform

    Screening was split across jobs, chat and CVs, so employers kept switching screens. I proposed a new navigation and screening workflow built around how employers already screen, with profile, CV and chat in one place, and owned the project as well as the design.

  • Self-serve subscription and quota

    Self-serve let inbound employers buy a plan themselves, not through Sales, and opened the platform to new ways to monetize. I designed the subscription (May 2025) and the monthly quota on it. In the A/B test, free employers who hit the limit were more than twice as likely to buy that month.

  • Chat to Apply

    Mobile traffic was growing, but intent wasn't turning into applications, and the resume was the first barrier. I designed chat as the first step and moved the CV to later. In Vietnam, 24–39% of chats led to a shortlist or a CV or phone exchange, against 7–10% of applications before chat.

  • Employer app

    Small business owners hire on their own, from their phone, and chat is a natural fit for mobile. I led the pilot as PM and designer, starting with existing employers and leaving sign-up and payment for later. Then I designed the move from web to app.

  • 1-Tap Apply

    For entry-level jobs, applying took many steps for little signal. I proposed one-tap apply over bulk or auto apply, to add applications without mass applying. It was A/B tested, then rolled out to all users.

  • Glints app in Vietnam

    In 2022, Glints took its app to a new market, Vietnam. I joined to launch it as both designer and PM: I designed the candidate app and the employer web, and defined the app's metrics.

# How I Work

Find the Problem Behind the Request

A layout complaint can hide a workflow problem. I use product data, A/B test results and interviews to find the real one before I design.

Decide What to Replace, Not Only What to Add

I write proposals with the problem, the evidence and the options, so the team can choose, and name the trade-off.

Design for How It's Built

I started as a front-end developer, so I check function, not only visuals, before release, and fix design debt in production through pull requests.

Build With AI

I've shipped a feature myself with AI coding agents, and built a Lark bot for user feedback and Storybook components that agents follow.
